1. Who can use KABAZA

You must be at least 18 years old to order alcohol, and old enough to enter a binding agreement in Malawi. You must provide a mobile number you control and a name that identifies you. One person may hold one passenger account.

2. Your account and phone verification

  • Your mobile number is your primary account identifier. We verify it with a one-time code sent by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Verification codes expire after 10 minutes and are limited in how often they can be requested and guessed.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password and verification codes private. Never share a code with anyone, including someone claiming to be KABAZA staff.
  • An email address is optional. If you do not give one, we use a phone-based internal identity — you do not need a Gmail or Google account.
  • If you believe someone else is using your account, contact us immediately using the details below.

3. Passengers and riders are different accounts

These terms cover passenger accounts. Riders and delivery partners go through a separate onboarding process with our operations team, including review of driving licence, vehicle and operational documents. Passenger registration does not grant rider access, and role changes are controlled by KABAZA administrators.

4. Identity documents

Ordinary passengers are not required to upload a national ID, passport or other government document to register. We may request additional verification only where required by law or where there is a clear safety or fraud-prevention reason, and we will tell you why. A profile photo is optional and helps your rider recognise you at the pickup point.

5. Orders, fares and payment

  • Fares and delivery fees shown in the app are estimates until confirmed. Final amounts are shown before you pay.
  • Payments are processed by PayChangu. KABAZA does not store your mobile money PIN or full payment credentials.
  • Alcohol orders are restricted to customers aged 18 and over. Riders may ask for proof of age on delivery and may refuse the delivery if it cannot be verified.
  • Every order is recorded in an internal provenance ledger for audit and dispute resolution.
