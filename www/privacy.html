Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · KABAZA, Salima, Malawi

We collect only what we need to run rides, deliveries and on-demand commerce in Salima. This page explains what we collect, who processes it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, mobile number (stored in international +265 format), optional email address, password (stored as a salted hash by our authentication provider), optional profile photo, and the town/area you choose.
  • Phone verification: the one-time code we send is stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash on our server with an expiry time. We never keep the plain-text code, never show it in the app, and never sell it.
  • Trip and order details: pickup and drop-off locations, parcel or order contents you enter, fare estimates, status updates, and messages or instructions relating to a booking.
  • Location: with your permission, the APK requests coarse or fine device location so riders can find you and so the app can suggest nearby service. You can deny the permission; the rest of the app still works, but location-based features may be unavailable.
  • Payment references: PayChangu transaction references, payment status and timestamps. KABAZA does not store your mobile money PIN or full payment card details.
  • Consent records: when you accepted the Terms and this Privacy Policy, and when you verified your phone number.

Ordinary passengers do not have to upload a national ID, passport, or other government document. Riders and delivery partners follow a separate onboarding process with additional checks.

2. Why we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and confirm your number belongs to you.
  • To match you with riders, show pickup locations, and keep you updated on rides, deliveries and orders.
  • To process and reconcile payments through PayChangu and keep an internal audit ledger for disputes and fraud prevention.
  • To communicate important service messages, such as verification codes and order status changes.
  • To improve safety and reliability, investigate misuse, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Who processes your information

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication and profile photo storage. Authentication and data access are governed by row-level security and server-side service credentials. Supabase infrastructure is primarily located outside Malawi.
  • PayChangu — processes mobile money and card payments. KABAZA receives payment status and a transaction reference, not your wallet PIN.
  • Messaging provider (SMS or WhatsApp Cloud API) — delivers your one-time verification code. We send only your number, the code, and the message or approved template content needed for verification.
  • Render — hosts the private KABAZA backend that creates payments and verifies OTPs. Secrets are stored in the server environment, never in the app.

These processors act on our instructions and may process data in other countries. Where a payment or message necessarily crosses borders, we take reasonable steps to protect the information in transit.

4. How long we keep information

  • Phone verification challenges and their hashes are removed automatically after 24 hours. A verified code token expires after 10 minutes.
  • Account and profile information is kept while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise it unless we must keep a record for legal, tax, safety or dispute-resolution reasons.
  • Ride, order and payment records are retained for our internal ledger and to handle disputes; we limit access to authorised staff.
  • Server and security logs are kept only as long as needed to diagnose problems and investigate abuse.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth. OTP codes are stored as salted hashes and rate-limited; a wrong code, an expired code, or a reused verification token is rejected. Secrets such as the PayChangu key, webhook secret and Supabase service-role key exist only on the server. Transport is encrypted with HTTPS, and row-level security restricts what the app can read or change.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can choose not to provide an email address. A phone number is enough for a passenger account.
  • You can choose not to add a profile photo, and you can deny location permission in your phone settings.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to stop optional processing. Some order and payment records may have to be retained for legal or dispute reasons.
  • You can sign out at any time using the sign-out button in the app (including the top bar of the APK).

To exercise any of these choices, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Children

KABAZA is not intended for children. Passenger accounts are for adults, and alcohol orders are strictly 18+.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service grows. The current version will always be available on this page and linked from the app. For privacy questions or requests, call or WhatsApp +265 123 456 789 or email support@kabaza.app. Our Terms are available on the Terms & Conditions page.
